King Me · Privacy policy · effective 3 September 2026

Your hops stay on your device

King Me is a kids’ checkers climb. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking pixels, and no account. This page is the policy Apple and Google ask for.

Who we are

The developer is Khepri Sun. Questions or a request to delete a nickname: ravioli2332@gmail.com.

What the app stores on this device

Your climb, Stars, sound and comfort toggles, and the nickname you type live in this phone’s app storage (or this browser). Deleting the app, or clearing the site’s data, erases that progress. We cannot restore it.

What leaves the device

Only if you pin a Daily Challenge score. Then we save the nickname you chose, the move count, and the time, on a Netlify server so today’s fewest-moves board can be shared. We do not ask for a real name, email, phone number, or location. The climb itself works with the radio off.

Children

The game is meant for families, including kids. We do not knowingly collect a child’s contact details. Nicknames are filtered for mean words. Anyone can hide a name on the board; hidden names drop off the shared list. If a child pinned a name you want gone, email us with the name and the UTC date of that daily board.

No selling, no ads, no tracking

We do not sell data. We do not run ads, analytics, or crash reporters. We do not use the Identifier for Advertisers. Standard HTTPS to our leaderboard is the only network call, and only for Daily Challenge.

How long scores stay

Each daily board is one UTC day. We may keep those lists so yesterday’s hops are still readable, and we may drop old days. Device saves stay until you clear them.

Your choices

  • Play the climb without ever typing a name.
  • Change the nickname on the title screen. New pins use the new name.
  • Hide a mean name on the leaderboard.
  • Email us to remove a pinned name.

Apple and Google labels

Data used to track you: none. Data linked to you: none. Data not linked to you: optional nickname and game score for the public daily board; gameplay progress that never leaves the device.
