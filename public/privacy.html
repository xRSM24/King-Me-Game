King Me · Privacy policy · effective 4 September 2026

Hops can stay on this phone, or follow you

King Me is a kids’ checkers climb. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no tracking pixels. An account is optional. This page is the policy Apple and Google ask for.

Who we are

The developer is Khepri Sun. Questions, a nickname takedown, or an account delete: ravioli2332@gmail.com.

What the app stores on this device

Your climb, Stars, sound and comfort toggles, hop history, and the nickname you type live in this phone’s app storage (or this browser). Deleting the app, or clearing the site’s data, erases the local copy. If you never made an account, we cannot restore it.

What leaves the device

A nickname leaves the device if you pin a Daily Challenge score or an Endless round count, or if you create an account. For a pin, we save the nickname, the move or round count, and the time on a Netlify server so the shared lists work.

Optional account: a grown-up email, a password hash (not the password), your nickname, Stars, a paused climb, and a hop log (boards cleared, Crowns, daily tries). That is so another phone can sign in and keep the same hops. We also keep anonymous counts (how many accounts, boards, Crowns) so we can see if the table is fun. Those counts are not tied to your email on the public site.

News mail: when someone starts a new keep-save, we ask if they want email about Daily and King Me news — that box starts checked. Uncheck it to keep hops only. They can turn news off later in Account, or email ravioli2332@gmail.com. We do not sell the list. It is developer mail, not ads.

We do not ask for a real name, phone number, or location. The climb itself works with the radio off. Accounts and Daily pins need a network.

Children

The game is meant for families, including kids. Play does not need an account. If a child wants hops to follow them, a parent or guardian should create the account with their own email. We do not knowingly collect a child’s contact details on purpose. Nicknames are filtered for mean words. Anyone can hide a name on the board; hidden names drop off the shared list. Email us to delete an account or a pinned name (include the UTC date for a daily pin).

No selling, no ads, no tracking

We do not sell data. We do not run ads, ad analytics, or crash reporters. We do not use the Identifier for Advertisers. Standard HTTPS to our hop book and leaderboard is the only network call.

How long it stays

Each daily board is one UTC day. We may keep those lists so yesterday’s hops are still readable, and we may drop old days. An account and its hop log stay until you delete the account in the app or email us. Device saves stay until you clear them.

Your choices

  • Play the climb without ever typing a name or creating an account.
  • Change the nickname on the title screen. New pins use the new name.
  • Create an account, sign out, or delete the account (password required).
  • Hide a mean name on the leaderboard.
  • Email us to remove a pinned name or a hop book.
  • Turn news mail on or off in Account, or email us. Uncheck when you keep a save if you only want hops.

Apple and Google labels

Data used to track you: none. Data linked to you: optional email and hop history if you create an account. Data not linked to you: optional nickname and game score for the public daily board; anonymous play counts; gameplay progress that stays on the device when you play without an account.
