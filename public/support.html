King Me · version 1.0.0

Need a hand?

This is a checkers climb. Six boards to the Crown, then pick a power. Today’s board is a shared puzzle with a fewest-moves list.

It works without the internet

The climb, Stars, Oops, and How to Play live on your phone. Daily Challenge scores need a network so everyone sees the same list. If the radio is off, you can still hop today’s felt; the pin waits on this device until you are back online — or stays here if the shared board cannot be reached.

Nothing to buy

No ads. No extra boards behind a paywall. No subscriptions.

Saves

Close the app mid-climb and Continue is waiting. Deleting the app deletes the climb. We do not keep a cloud copy of your run.
