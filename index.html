Super simple

How to Play

  1. Hold WASD to run. Your costume gun auto-fires — pins, fans, shotguns, rings — and leans toward the nearest costume. Just steer.
  2. No hearts. Bonks knock costumes off the pile. Last one gone = sent home.
  3. One sandbox, one trail. Side rooms have snacks and shops. The main path gets meaner the closer you get to King Empty. First tries should send you home five to ten times before you even see him.
  4. Wear or Snack. Wear = put the outfit on (and use its gun). Snack = coins, but King Empty keeps that costume.
  5. XP is split on purpose. Kills level your gun this life only. Getting sent home turns leftover XP into Spark, which permanently buffs guns — but Spark has diminishing returns and a hard ceiling, so later deaths help less and you never steamroll the trail.
