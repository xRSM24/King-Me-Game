Soulstack

You have no health. You are a pile of faces. Hits tear them off. The ones you refuse to wear wait for you at the bottom of the dungeon.

How a stack survives

The Rule

  1. No hit points. You are the souls you wear. A hit peels the top one off. You wake with one stitch — a blow the thread can catch. When the last face goes, you end.
  2. Bump to kill. WASD or arrows move. Space uses your current soul's power. Z waits.
  3. Wear or Harvest. Wear them and you become them — buried lives grant echoes. Harvest them for gold. Every harvest feeds the Hollow.
  4. The stack is short. Overflow, and the bottom life falls into the Hollow's grave.
  5. Resonances. Certain lives sung together change the rules. Finding them is the reason to descend again.
  6. The Hollow. Floor 6. It is made of the faces you sold. Greedy runs make a worse god.
