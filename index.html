    How to Play

    1. Slide, or tap. Drag your piece over the Enemy onto the star. That is a jump — you capture them. You can also drop onto the Enemy. Quiet slides go onto a cream pip. You can still tap the piece, then the spot. A black pit cannot be sat on — jump over it onto the star past it.
    2. If you can jump, you must. Keep jumping with the same checker if another star appears. Reach the far row — the edge opposite the ranks you started on — to become a King. Landing in your own starting ranks never makes a King. A King steps 1 square on any diagonal. Super King and Long King ride the empty diagonal — up to 7 squares, not 1. Skip the Jump adds a Skip jump button so you can walk 1 square instead — or stop a combo.
    3. No endless chase. When only Kings are left, if nobody jumps for 8 hops, whoever has more pieces wins. A lone King cannot run forever.
    4. Six boards to the Crown. Each New climb rolls a new path — different files, different felt, different names. Continue is the same climb you paused.
    5. Oops is OK. One take-back per climb board. Wait for the Enemy to hop, see what happened, then Oops if you want your hop back. Today's tough board gives you two unless a modifier says otherwise. If you lose your last piece on the climb, you still get Stars.
    6. Today's board is shared. You and your friends hop the same hard felt until midnight UTC. Everyone testing King Me sits on one fewest-moves list. Two Oops unless a daily twist says otherwise. Each day rolls a few modifiers — some help you, some help the Enemy. Win it, pin your move count. Lowest moves sits on top.
    7. Your name. Type it on the title and tap That's me. It sits on today's leaderboard. No email — just a nickname on this device. Mean words are blocked. Tap Hide on a name that should not be there.
    8. Offline. The climb works with the radio off. Daily pins need a network so everyone shares one list. Nothing to buy. No ads.
    9. Keys and comfort. Arrows move the gold outline. Enter hops. Escape opens Menu. Colorblind paints your maple disc gold and the Enemy pawn teal. Motion off skips hops and petals.

    Privacy

    Your hops stay on this device

    No ads, no purchases, no tracking. The climb, Stars, and the nickname you type live on this phone. Deleting the app erases them. We cannot restore a climb.

    A nickname leaves the device only if you pin a Daily Challenge score. Then we keep that name, the move count, and the time so today’s fewest-moves board can be shared. We never ask for a real name, email, or location.

    Mean words are blocked. Hide hides a name for you right away and takes it off the shared list. To delete a pin, write ravioli2332@gmail.com with the name and the UTC date.
