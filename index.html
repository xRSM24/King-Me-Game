Goal every run: hop the path to the Black Crown. English draughts, then the laws change. You will be wiped. Notches make the next opening a little kinder — and then they stop, so you never own the table.

The house rules

How to Play

  1. Ivory is yours. Charcoal is the house. Men hop forward on the dark squares. Capture by jumping. If a jump is on the table, you must take it.
  2. The last row crowns you. Kings hop any diagonal. Multi-jumps stay with the same piece.
  3. Six felts to the Black Crown. Each win you pick a Law that rewrites the rest of the run. Later boards field more charcoal than notches can ever match.
  4. Empty felt is death. Lose every piece and the run is over. Captures become Notches, with diminishing returns and a hard ceiling.
