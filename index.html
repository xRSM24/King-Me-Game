    How to Play

    1. Slide, or tap. Drag a gold-ring checker onto a cream pip. Stars are jumps — those capture! You can still tap the piece, then the spot.
    2. If you can jump, you must. Keep jumping with the same checker if another star appears. Reach the far row to become a king.
    3. Six boards to the Crown. Each one plays a little differently. Win, pick a power, keep hopping.
    4. Oops is OK on the climb. One take-back per board. If you lose your last checker, you still get Stars. Sit down again!
    5. Today's board is a toughie. Everyone gets the same hard felt until midnight UTC. Win it, pin your move count. Lowest moves sits on top.
