A goofy costume-pile adventure

Soulstack

You are Pip. You have no hearts — only a pile of silly costumes. Bonks knock them off. Outfits you skip go to King Empty. Don't skip too many.

Super simple

How to Play

  1. No hearts. Pip is a pile of costumes. A bonk knocks the top one off. You start with one Lucky Pin (a free save). When the last costume pops, you go home.
  2. Bump to boop. WASD or arrows move. Space uses your costume power. Z waits.
  3. Wear or Snack. Wear = put their outfit on. Snack = take coins, but King Empty keeps the costume for the final fight.
  4. The pile is short. Too many outfits and the bottom one falls into the King's closet.
  5. Combo outfits. Some costumes together make a silly combo. That's the fun of coming back.
  6. King Empty. Floor 6. A giant raincoat made of every outfit you didn't wear. Greedy snack runs make a bigger king.
