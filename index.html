How to Play

  1. Slide or jump. Slide 1 square onto a cream spot. When you can jump an Enemy, a gold star appears on the square you would land on. Land on that star to hop over them and capture. You can slide onto a spot instead. A black pit cannot be sat on — land on the star past it to jump over. Tap the piece, then the square, if you like.
  2. Jumps are a choice. You never have to jump. After you capture, you may jump again, slide onto a spot, or tap Skip jump. Reach the far row to become a King. A King steps 1 square on any diagonal.
  3. Six boards to the Crown. New climb is a new path. Continue is the same climb you paused. Endless keeps going if you keep winning. You hold three treats, then swap one after each later board.
  4. Name and Stars. Type a name on the title if you want it on today's Daily list and the Endless list. Captures become Stars — a little boost for the next First Hop, even if you lose.

Privacy

Hops can follow you

No ads, no purchases, no ad tracking. Play without an account and the climb stays on this phone. An optional grown-up email keeps Stars and hop history if you switch devices.

A nickname leaves the device if you pin a Daily Challenge score or an Endless round count, or if you create an account. Then we keep that name, the email, a password hash, the climb, Stars, and a hop log so you can sign in later. News mail starts on for a new keep-save unless you uncheck the box. Turn it off in Account or email us. We do not sell the list. It is developer mail, not ads. We never ask for a real name, phone, or location.

Mean words are blocked. Hide hides a name for you right away and takes it off the shared list. To delete a pin, write ravioli2332@gmail.com with the name and the UTC date.
