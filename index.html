    How to Play

    1. Slide, or tap. Drag a gold-ring checker onto a cream pip. Stars are jumps — those capture! You can still tap the piece, then the spot.
    2. If you can jump, you must. Keep jumping with the same checker if another star appears. Reach the far row — the edge opposite the ranks you started on — to become a King. Landing in your own starting ranks never makes a King. A King steps 1 square on any diagonal. Super King and Long King ride the empty diagonal — up to 7 squares, not 1. Skip the Jump adds a Skip jump button so you can walk 1 square instead — or stop a combo.
    3. Six boards to the Crown. Each New climb rolls a new path — different files, different felt, different names. Continue is the same climb you paused.
    4. Oops is OK. One take-back per climb board. Wait for the Enemy to hop, see what happened, then Oops if you want your hop back. Today's tough board gives you two unless a modifier says otherwise. If you lose your last piece on the climb, you still get Stars.
    5. Today's board is shared. You and your friends hop the same hard felt until midnight UTC. Two Oops unless a daily twist says otherwise. Each day rolls a few modifiers — some help you, some help the Enemy. Win it, pin your move count. Lowest moves sits on top.
    6. Your name. Type it on the title and tap That's me. It sits on today's leaderboard. No email yet — just a name on this device.
    7. Keys and comfort. Arrows move the gold outline. Enter hops. Escape opens Menu. Colorblind paints player pieces round-and-gold and Enemy pieces square-and-teal. Motion off skips hops and petals.
