How to Play

  1. Hold WASD to run. Bump a costume to boop it. Space uses your outfit's trick.
  2. No hearts. Bonks knock costumes off the pile. Last one gone = sent home.
  3. Each closet has a job. Boop enough costumes and a glowing green hole pops open. Run onto it for the next closet.
  4. Wear or Snack. Wear = put the outfit on. Snack = coins, but King Empty keeps that costume.
  5. The real goal is far. King Empty waits in closet 10. First tries will send you home. Spend Stickers between runs. That's a roguelike.
